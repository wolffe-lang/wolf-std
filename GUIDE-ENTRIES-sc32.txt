## 2026-09-02 (sc32) — the budget has a shape

- A CAPABILITY with no carrier moves nothing, and that is a
  measurement only if you grep for it. sc31's empty drift list came
  from a span that added no surface; sc32's came from the span that
  added the biggest memory surface since regions themselves — the byte
  ledger, the creation-time cap, the contained fault — and it is still
  empty, because this repo had never asked the question the new
  surface answers. The two zeros read identically and mean opposite
  things: one says "nothing could have moved", the other says "a
  sprint's worth of work is sitting in front of you". Write which one
  you have. And never let "additive" stand in for the check: the grep
  that found no `region_bytes`, no `cap:` and no `spawn` in the tree is
  the evidence, not the word.

- PROBE THE SIGNATURE YOU MEAN TO SHIP, ONE PROPERTY AT A TIME, BEFORE
  THE FIRST LINE. `std.mem.budget` cost fifteen probes and five of them
  changed the module. The two that would have been guessed wrong in
  both directions are the pair worth remembering. A `region` in
  ARGUMENT position is RETAINED by the caller — region values are
  affine, `[mem.region.create.2]` says they move and are never copied,
  and a reader is right to expect `charged(r)` to eat its argument; it
  does not, on any lane, because `[mem.tier0.mode.read]` says the
  unwritten mode leaves the caller holding the value, and nobody had
  ever tested that sentence against a region. But a region may not be
  RETURNED: the native rung refuses `-> region` by name ("first-class
  region values beyond local bindings"). Taking and returning are not
  one question, and the clause that licenses one says nothing about
  the other.

- WHEN A HELPER CANNOT HAND BACK A VALUE, FIND OUT WHETHER THAT IS A
  LIMIT OR THE CONTRACT. The obvious signature was
  `with_cap[T](n, f) -> T ! {exhausted}`, and three measurements closed
  the door: a proc has no shared address space beyond its channels
  (`[conc.proc.1]`), a `channel[int]` in a std SIGNATURE is refused on
  both wolf rungs, and one kept inside a std BODY is refused on the
  checked one. The right response was not to mourn the signature but
  to notice that the contract had already ruled: `[conc.proc.kill]`
  bulk-frees the proc's regions before the reason delivers, so nothing
  the work ALLOCATED could have survived under any spelling. Only a
  scalar was ever crossing — which is the discipline the consumer's own
  request path already wrote by hand. A limit that agrees with the
  contract is a design, and saying so in the header is what stops the
  next lane from re-litigating it.

- A TIER'S REFUSAL MAY BE REACHED AT EXECUTION RATHER THAN DECIDED
  STATICALLY, and that changes what a module costs. The checked tier
  answers "structured concurrency in checked execution (C1 deferred)"
  for the task layer — so a module built on procs looks two-lane. It is
  not: a `with_cap` call behind a runtime-false branch compiles and
  runs to `exit(0)` there, and one of this module's three witnesses is
  `run` on all three lanes because its guard traps before the `spawn`
  is reached. So the same function yields two different checked columns
  in one ledger, neither of them a mistake, and a caller may link the
  module on the checked tier and pay only for the paths that spawn.
  Before you write a module off as two-lane, find out whether the
  refusal is a verdict on the program or on the step.

- WHEN THE UNITS ARE PER-TIER, THE TEST PRINTS RELATIONS. The region
  ledger's numbers are implementation-measured facts and explicitly not
  comparison surface — the same 100-element list reads 4064 under
  lupin, 2032 native and 1600 checked — and this rig proves behaviour
  with a stdout hash compared ACROSS lanes. Those two facts decide the
  test design between them: print `grew`/`stable`/`reclaimed`, never a
  count, and one hash covers every lane. It is the same reasoning
  wolf-lang's own three-tier cap witnesses use to breach on every tier
  from one file, and it is worth copying a corpus witness's exact shape
  when the shape is the part that was measured — the attribution half
  of ours is written the way `memory/region_bytes_value.lu` writes it
  because the other way is E1010 on both wolf rungs.

- A ROW WITH NO PAYLOAD IS SOMETIMES THE CONTRACT SPEAKING, not
  minimalism. `exhausted` carries no number because
  `[mem.region.cap.3]` makes teardown free-then-deliver: the breaching
  proc's regions are reclaimed wholesale before the reason reaches the
  join, so the charge a reader would want in the payload is
  UNOBSERVABLE by contract and would have to be invented. The same
  clause is why the row is safe to act on at all — the supervisor that
  answers 503 was handed the memory back first. And where the row and
  the trap rule collide, the trap rule wins: a negative budget would
  have ridden out as `exhausted` (the language's own trap fires inside
  the proc and is contained), so std checks it at the door, because a
  caller's arithmetic mistake answered with a recoverable value is the
  exact failure the trap rule exists to prevent.

- MEASURE A UPSTREAM ASK FROM YOUR OWN SIDE BEFORE YOU SECOND IT. #203
  reported a 16x byte-buffer cost from one consumer; this repo
  reproduced both numbers to the byte from a different program at a
  later pin, showed the ratio is flat from 1 KiB to 64 KiB, and turned
  up a multiplier the issue did not have (the reference interpreter is
  32x, because its value slot is twice the compiled tiers'). An
  independent reproduction turns "our server sees this" into "the
  representation is this", and the extra column is what tells a
  portable program not to hard-code a per-tier constant. The
  measurement also found something nobody asked for — a `str` charges
  NO named region on ANY tier, where the clause scopes that gap to one
  — which is the ordinary dividend of writing the probe rather than
  citing the issue.

- A RESIDUE'S METHOD HAS TO SURVIVE THE SPAN THAT CONTRADICTS IT. Five
  bumps running, `strbuf.in(r)` was re-dated from the commit list:
  "nothing in this span touches regions". This span touched regions
  more than any since regions landed, so the sentence had to become a
  probe — and the probe says the placement syntax is absent on every
  lane and `fail(E0201)` at PARSE for the struct form. The residue did
  not move, but the reason it did not move is now measured rather than
  inherited. When the span finally does touch your residue's area, that
  is the bump where the argument stops being allowed to stand in for
  the measurement.
