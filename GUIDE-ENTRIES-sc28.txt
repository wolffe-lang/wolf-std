## 2026-08-30 (sc28) — the library writes the new words; a rung sweeps a corpus

- When a release lands a STATIC RUNG, predict its footprint by the
  rung's SHAPE, not by the issues it closes: sc28 predicted the four
  filed `divergent(…)` carriers of is29's take-mode fix and measured
  SIX movers — the same fail(E1001) rung also reached the corpus's two
  unfiled take-then-reuse witnesses (fs/use_after_close, deeper: the
  static rejection answers BEFORE the machine's by-design fs decline;
  sha2's finished-digest witness, whose directive had to modernize).
  A dynamic divergence lives in filed rows; a static analysis sweeps
  every program with the shape. Grep for the shape first.
- A directive can lawfully MODERNIZE when a machine changes rungs, and
  the name moves with it: `run(exit=trap(use-after-move))` described a
  two-rung world (interpreter dynamic, compilers static); when is29
  put all three lanes on the static rung, no lupin ledger word could
  spell fail-under-a-trap-directive, so the directive became
  fail(E1001), all three lanes read `run` against it, the two-rung
  history stayed in the file header — and lint-conventions FORCED the
  rename (`use_after_final_trap.lu` -> `use_after_final.lu`): a
  `…_trap.lu` name is a promise (§13), and the rig caught its own
  paperwork the moment the directive stopped making it.
- D57 is COMPLETE and the acquisition ritual shrank to one line: wolf
  v0.2.0's line 1 names its own pin (`wolf 0.2.0 (wolfgang, pin
  c88ab64)`; off-tag builds self-brand `+dev`), so provenance is the
  binary's own word on BOTH tools now — the sc24-era behavioral-witness
  ritual and doctor's "trusted from acquisition" WARN retired together,
  and a binary that names no pin is a doctor RED (older than every
  recorded pin), no longer a shrug.
- A ledger word can retire to ZERO carriers and keep its plumbing: the
  four `divergent(…)` rows flipped to `run` at 0.1.18 (each observed as
  the runner's designed red at the heal, each re-measured from its
  staged dir with compiler span parity), and the mechanism stays armed
  per the sc25 waiver precedent — price any future entry at a named
  finding. Four sprints of byte-identical re-measures are what made the
  flip a one-line read.
- When the anchor prices two spellings EQUAL, the choice becomes a
  reading, and std spells it by ROLE: `[type.str.concat.cost]` makes
  D62's `+`/`+=` the same lowering as `"{a}{b}"`, so the operator won
  every pure two-str join and self-append (23 of 27 sites), while
  interpolation KEPT the sites where a hole converts (`char`/int —
  `str + char` is E0409 by name, so the hole is load-bearing) and every
  3+-piece join. State the split where you adopt (§9 amendment) — a
  reader should never wonder why one site kept the old spelling.
- Destructure where the tuple is BORN: `let (q, r) = …` replaces
  paired `.0`/`.1` locals at the call that makes the pair, and a
  function called twice for its halves consolidates to one binding.
  But a for-bound element keeps `.0`/`.1` — a loop visits a tuple, it
  does not bind it anew — and `wolf fmt` arbitrates D63 binder groups
  the same way: adopt a comma group only where a run of bindings is
  one thought AND fmt keeps it one line; a group fmt breaks multi-line
  was never one thought.
- A slice is the language's own word for the hand-rolled sub-list
  copy: 12 index loops at 9 sites became ranges, each priced against
  the fresh-copy anchor first (`b[from..to]` copies exactly what the
  loop pushed), and the rule cuts THREE ways — a loop that avoids the
  copy on purpose stays a loop (say so in a comment), and a function
  whose parameter can arrive LENT keeps the loop too: `bytes.slice`
  adopted the range, the gauntlet moved exactly one row
  (`lend_across_calls` native, `run` -> `unsupported` — a slice of a
  lent view refuses at `mem` on the native rung while every owned
  caller compiles; F-0101), and the site retreated the same day.
  "Any row that moves under an adoption commit is a finding, not a
  shrug" is not a slogan: the rig caught the one-row regression a
  five-file adoption smuggled in, and the ledger stayed the arbiter.
  Adoption is provable style — the families landed with ZERO verdict
  motion over the full ledger.
- A residue re-probe is one line when the refusal is verbatim: the
  chars-pairs tuple list (`List[(int, int)]`) re-measured `unsupported`
  at resolve at v0.2.0/c88ab64 — same prelude-container-instantiation
  word, third consecutive pin, dated in the str header where the fused
  `chars()` waits. Measure, date, move on.
